Register a username and password. The username can be registered only once on this device — after that, you'll log in with it.

Privacy, Security & Risk Disclosure

Please read and respond before continuing. You must agree to all items, or you can decline and exit.

This is a paper-trading (simulated) app. It does not ask for your bank login, card, or banking passwords, and it cannot move real money. All trading happens with simulated funds.

Data & security

Your login and account state are kept only in this browser on this device, and are never sent to Anthropic or any server. Anyone with access to this computer could view them, so use a device you trust.

Investment risk

The moving-average momentum strategy used here can lose money. The app will keep trading toward your target, but reaching it is not guaranteed and equity can fall. Nothing here is financial advice.

Set up your account

Enter how much (simulated) cash you're funding the account with, and the target value you want the auto-trader to reach. It will trade automatically until your portfolio hits the target, then stop.
