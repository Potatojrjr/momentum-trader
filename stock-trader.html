Privacy, Security & Risk Disclosure

Before you use this app, please read and respond to the following. You must agree to all items to continue, or you can decline and close the app.

This is a paper-trading (simulated) application. It does not ask for your bank login, debit/credit card, or banking passwords, and it cannot move real money. It connects only to a brokerage paper (sandbox) account using API keys you generate yourself. No real funds are ever at risk.

What data this app touches

Your Alpaca paper API key & secret are kept only in your browser's memory for this session and are sent directly to the broker's servers to fetch your simulated account. They are never sent to Anthropic, never stored on disk, and disappear when you close the tab.

Security concerns you should know

Even paper API keys should be treated as secrets — never paste real live trading keys here. Browsers can be inspected by anyone with access to your computer. Use this only on a device you trust.

Investment risk

Algorithmic strategies, including the moving-average momentum strategy used here, can and do lose money. Past performance does not predict future results. Nothing in this app is financial advice. This tool is for education and experimentation with simulated funds only.

Connect your brokerage paper account

Enter your Alpaca paper trading API keys. Create free keys at Alpaca's paper dashboard (look for "Generate API Keys" under the Paper Trading account).

Browsers sometimes block direct calls to brokerage APIs (CORS). If the connection fails, the app automatically falls back to a fully-featured local simulation so you can still try everything.
